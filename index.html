Why I do this

For my step-dad — and for yours

When my step-dad died, I lost the kindest, gentlest man I have ever known. His funeral had what every funeral has — a few speakers, and a basic paid slideshow. Thirty photographs. No story.

I wanted to stand up and do him justice, but my anxiety got the better of me, and the words I managed were a pale reflection of the greatest man I ever met. What I could do was make films.

Those thirty photos could have been his whole life, cradle to grave — with a voice for those of us who couldn't find ours on the hardest day. That's why BlueDoorClips exists: so no one's story goes untold.

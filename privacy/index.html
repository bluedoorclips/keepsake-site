Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 August 2026

Families trust us with photographs of people they have just lost. We treat that material as the private, irreplaceable thing it is. This page explains, in plain English, what we hold, why, and for how long.

Who we are

BlueDoorClips Limited makes funeral tribute films, memorial slideshow templates and printable funeral stationery from Scotland, serving families and funeral directors across the UK. We are the data controller for the information described here. You can reach us at bluedoorclips@gmail.com for anything on this page, including any request to see or delete what we hold.

What we collect, and why

  • Photographs, video clips and the details you send us — names, dates, service details, music choices and any wording you supply. We use these solely to make the film, slideshow, invitation or card set you have ordered.
  • Order and contact details — your name, email address and messages. Where you order through Etsy, Etsy handles your payment and shares only what we need to fulfil the order; we never see your card details.
  • Memory Card contributions — where you buy a Memory Card, guests at the service can scan its code and upload photographs, video clips and written memories. These are collected for the family who bought the card, and are given to them alone.

Our lawful basis is the performance of our contract with you. Where material includes special-category information — a photograph of a person, a religious element in a service — we rely on your explicit consent, given by sending it to us for that purpose. You may withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us.

How long we keep things

  • Family photographs and clips — deleted within 30 days of delivering your finished film, unless you ask us to keep them so that changes can be made later.
  • Memory Card pages — each page stays open for 60 days from your order. We then send everything gathered to the family and remove it from our servers within 30 days.
  • Order records — kept for six years, as UK tax law requires. These are records of the transaction, not your photographs.

Who else sees it

We do not sell your information, and we never share it for advertising. Nothing you send is published, posted to social media or used as an example of our work without your written permission. A small number of service providers process data on our behalf so the service can work at all:

  • Etsy — where you place an order and message us.
  • Railway — hosts this website and, where applicable, your private tribute or memory page.
  • Google (Gmail) — our email.

Memory Card pages and private tribute pages are unlisted and carry instructions to search engines not to index them. They are reachable only by the code or link given to the family.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. Write to bluedoorclips@gmail.com and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with our answer you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address in order to serve them.

Children

Our services are sold to adults. Photographs of children often appear in tribute films, sent to us by their family; we treat them exactly as we treat everything else here, and delete them on the timetable above.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
